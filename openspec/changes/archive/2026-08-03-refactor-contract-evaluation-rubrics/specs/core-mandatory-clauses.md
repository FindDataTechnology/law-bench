## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Core Mandatory Clauses for Civil Code Named Contracts
The system SHALL define core mandatory clauses for each of the 19 Civil Code typical named contracts. These clauses represent the essential provisions required by the Civil Code for each contract type.

#### Civil Code Named Contracts (19 types):

**1. Sale Contract (买卖合同)** - Chapter 9, Articles 595-647
Core mandatory clauses:
- 标的物 (Subject matter)
- 数量 (Quantity)
- 质量 (Quality)
- 价款 (Price)
- 履行期限/地点/方式 (Time/place/method of performance)
- 违约责任 (Breach liability)
- 争议解决 (Dispute resolution)

**2. Supply/Electric/Water/Gas/Heat Contract (供用电水气热力合同)** - Chapter 10, Articles 648-656
Core mandatory clauses:
- 供电/供水/供气/供热种类 (Type of supply)
- 质量和标准 (Quality and standards)
- 数量和计量方式 (Quantity and measurement)
- 价格和收费办法 (Price and charging method)
- 供电/供水/供气/供热时间 (Supply time)
- 设施维护责任 (Facility maintenance responsibility)
- 违约责任 (Breach liability)

**3. Gift Contract (赠与合同)** - Chapter 11, Articles 657-666
Core mandatory clauses:
- 赠与财产 (Gift property)
- 赠与目的 (Purpose of gift)
- 交付方式 (Delivery method)
- 撤销权情形 (Grounds for revocation)

**4. Loan Contract (借款合同)** - Chapter 12, Articles 667-680
Core mandatory clauses:
- 借款种类 (Loan type)
- 币种 (Currency)
- 用途 (Purpose)
- 金额 (Amount)
- 利率 (Interest rate)
- 期限 (Term)
- 还款方式 (Repayment method)

**5. Guarantee Contract (保证合同)** - Chapter 13, Articles 681-702
Core mandatory clauses:
- 被担保债权的种类和数额 (Nature and amount of guaranteed claim)
- 债务人履行债务的期限 (Deadline for debtor's performance)
- 保证的方式 (Guarantee mode: general or joint)
- 担保的范围 (Scope of guarantee)
- 保证期间 (Guarantee period)

**6. Lease Contract (租赁合同)** - Chapter 14, Articles 703-734
Core mandatory clauses:
- 租赁物名称 (Leased property name)
- 数量 (Quantity)
- 用途 (Purpose)
- 租赁期限 (Lease term)
- 租金 (Rent)
- 租金支付期限和方式 (Payment terms)
- 维修义务 (Maintenance obligation)

**7. Financing Lease Contract (融资租赁合同)** - Chapter 15, Articles 735-760
Core mandatory clauses:
- 租赁物名称、数量、规格 (Leased property details)
- 技术性能 (Technical specifications)
- 检验方法 (Inspection method)
- 租金构成及支付方式 (Rent composition and payment)
- 租赁期满租赁物归属 (Ownership at lease end)

**8. Factoring Contract (保理合同)** - Chapter 16, Articles 761-769
Core mandatory clauses:
- 业务范围和服务范围 (Business and service scope)
- 应收账款信息 (Receivables information)
- 保理融资款数额和支付方式 (Factoring financing amount)
- 服务报酬及支付方式 (Service fee)
- 保理商追索权 (Factor's right of recourse)

**9. Work Contract (承揽合同)** - Chapter 17, Articles 770-787
Core mandatory clauses:
- 承揽标的 (Contract subject)
- 数量、质量 (Quantity and quality)
- 材料提供 (Material provision)
- 报酬 (Remuneration)
- 履行期限 (Performance term)
- 验收标准和方法 (Acceptance criteria and method)

**10. Construction Contract (建设工程合同)** - Chapter 18, Articles 788-808
Core mandatory clauses:
- 工程范围 (Engineering scope)
- 建设工期 (Construction period)
- 中间交工工程的开工和竣工日期 (Start/end dates for intermediate works)
- 工程质量 (Project quality)
- 工程造价 (Project cost)
- 技术资料交付时间 (Technical data delivery time)
- 材料和设备供应责任 (Materials/equipment supply responsibility)
- 拨款和结算 (Fund allocation and settlement)
- 竣工验收 (Completion acceptance)

**11. Transport Contract (运输合同)** - Chapter 19, Articles 809-842
Core mandatory clauses:
- 起运地 (Place of departure)
- 目的地 (Destination)
- 承运人 (Carrier)
- 旅客/货物名称 (Passenger/goods name)
- 运费 (Freight)
- 运输期限 (Transport term)

**12. Technology Contract (技术合同)** - Chapter 20, Articles 843-887
Includes subtypes: Development, Transfer, License, Consultation, Service

Core mandatory clauses vary by subtype, typically including:
- 项目名称/内容/范围 (Project name/content/scope)
- 履行计划 (Performance plan)
- 技术要求 (Technical requirements)
- 保密义务 (Confidentiality)
- 风险承担 (Risk bearing)
- 成果归属 (Ownership of results)
- 验收标准 (Acceptance criteria)
- 报酬 (Remuneration)

**13. Bailment Contract (保管合同)** - Chapter 21, Articles 888-903
Core mandatory clauses:
- 保管物 (Bailment object)
- 保管场所 (Storage place)
- 保管期间 (Storage period)
- 保管费用 (Storage fee)
- 返还方式 (Return method)

**14. Warehousing Contract (仓储合同)** - Chapter 22, Articles 904-918
Core mandatory clauses:
- 储存物品种类/性质 (Stored goods type/nature)
- 包装要求 (Packaging requirements)
- 验收项目和方式 (Acceptance items and method)
- 损耗标准 (Loss standard)
- 储存期限 (Storage period)
- 费用和结算 (Fee and settlement)
- 危险物品处理 (Dangerous goods handling)

**15. Entrustment Contract (委托合同)** - Chapter 23, Articles 919-936
Core mandatory clauses:
- 委托事项 (Entrusted matters)
- 权限范围 (Scope of authority)
- 费用预付 (Advance expenses)
- 报酬 (Remuneration)
- 报告义务 (Reporting obligation)
- 转委托 (Sub-entrustment)

**16. Property Service Contract (物业服务合同)** - Chapter 24, Articles 937-950
Core mandatory clauses:
- 服务内容和服务标准 (Service content and standards)
- 服务费用 (Service fees)
- 专项维修资金的使用 (Use of maintenance fund)
- 服务用房的管理 (Management of service space)
- 服务期限 (Service period)
- 违约责任 (Breach liability)

**17. Brokerage Contract (行纪合同)** - Chapter 25, Articles 951-960
Core mandatory clauses:
- 行纪标的物 (Brokerage subject)
- 价格指示 (Price instruction)
- 行纪报酬 (Brokerage remuneration)
- 处置义务 (Disposal obligation)

**18. Intermediation Contract (中介合同)** - Chapter 26, Articles 961-966
Core mandatory clauses:
- 中介服务项目 (Intermediation project)
- 费用负担 (Expense burden)
- 报酬 (Remuneration)
- 如实报告义务 (Duty to report truthfully)

**19. Partnership Contract (合伙合同)** - Chapter 27, Articles 967-978
Core mandatory clauses:
- 合伙人出资 (Partner contributions)
- 利润分配和亏损分担 (Profit distribution and loss sharing)
- 事务执行 (Execution of affairs)
- 入伙退伙 (Admission/withdrawal)
- 解散清算 (Dissolution and liquidation)

### Requirement: Core Mandatory Clauses for Unnamed Contracts
The system SHALL define core mandatory clauses for each unnamed contract based on its governing statutes beyond the Civil Code. Each clause category SHALL reference the specific legal basis.

- 股东权利义务 (Shareholder rights and obligations)

**Equity Incentive (股权激励协议)**

- 触发条件 (Trigger conditions)

**Equity Holding in Trust (股权代持协议)**

- 名义股东义务 (Nominal shareholder duties)
- 显名条件 (Conditions for disclosure)
- 风险承担 (Risk bearing)

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Evaluation Criteria Mapping
For each contract type, the system SHALL maintain a mapping between core mandatory clauses and evaluation criteria. This allows the evaluation engine to determine which criteria should be applied during contract review.

#### Scenario: Map sale contract clauses to criteria
- **WHEN** evaluating a sale contract
- **THEN** the system SHALL map clauses to: party identification, governing law, ownership transfer, risk transfer, inspection period, defect warranty, etc.
- **AND** mark missing critical clauses as failures with specific feedback

#### Scenario: Map employment contract clauses to criteria
- **WHEN** evaluating an employment contract
- **THEN** the system SHALL map clauses to: labor compensation, working hours, social insurance, termination conditions, economic compensation, etc.
- **AND** prioritize labor law mandatory provisions as pass-fail gates
